# Australian Structural Verification Basis

## Decision

NCC 2022 and the project-selected Australian standards are the primary design
and certification basis. SCI Publication P399, *Design of steel portal frame
buildings to Eurocode 3*, is supplemental portal-frame analysis guidance only.

P399 contributes an audit-friendly order of work, the analytical questions that must be
answered, and the expected relationship between frame analysis, stability,
member verification, restraint, connections, bases, and serviceability. It is
not the compliance framework and is not an instruction to apply UK actions,
factors, material rules, or section resistances to an Australian project.

Australian actions, combinations, materials, cold-formed member rules,
connections, anchors, concrete, manufacturer data, and project requirements
must be supplied by a separately versioned Australian standards pack. Every
calculation result must record both, in this order:

- the Australian standard, manufacturer source, project criterion, or explicit
  engineering assumption that supplies the actual inputs and resistance rule;
- any supplemental P399 process stage or source section that helps explain why
  the check exists.

## Australian verification sequence with supplemental P399 mapping

The Australian verification workflow uses the sequence below. P399 Table 3.1
and Sections 4 to 12 are recorded as supplemental process references. Tertius must
not colour a member as passing until every preceding stage required for that
check has a valid result.

| Stage | Supplemental P399 basis | Required Tertius output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Geometry and structural scheme | 3, 6.1 | Current physical geometry, analytical centre-lines, supports, releases, offsets, member roles, openings, and stable source IDs. |
| 2. Actions | 4 | Permanent, imposed, wind, construction, accidental, and other applicable actions with tributary areas, direction, application points, and provenance. |
| 3. Combinations | 4.7 | Explicit ULS and SLS combinations generated by the selected Australian standards pack. Mutually exclusive actions remain exclusive. Favourable permanent actions can be omitted where required, including uplift cases. |
| 4. Analysis method | 5 | Declared elastic, plastic, first-order, or second-order method. The initial shed implementation uses elastic analysis unless a later design basis explicitly justifies plastic analysis and its ductility/restraint requirements. |
| 5. Imperfections and global stability | 7.2-7.8 | Equivalent/notional horizontal actions or explicit imperfections, base-stiffness model, elastic critical factor or direct second-order result, amplification decision, convergence, and instability diagnostics. |
| 6. Cross-section verification | 8.1 | Section classification/effective properties and axial, shear, bending, torsion, and applicable interaction checks at governing stations. |
| 7. Member stability | 8.2-8.4 | Restraint-defined segments, compression flange for each load direction, flexural buckling, lateral-torsional/distortional/local buckling as applicable, and axial-bending interaction. |
| 8. Bracing and restraint | 9 | Vertical and roof bracing load paths, purlin/girt restraint assumptions, torsional restraints, opening interruptions, force demand, stiffness, and continuity to a resisting system. |
| 9. Connections and bases | 11 | Load-reversing connection actions, fasteners, plates/brackets, bearing/tear-out/net section, base shear/uplift/moment, anchors, concrete and foundation interface, with explicit responsibility boundaries. |
| 10. Serviceability | 12 | Elastic SLS deflections and movements for the applicable combinations, including absolute and differential criteria selected for the actual cladding, walls, openings, doors, and client requirements. |
| 11. Evidence and decision | whole process | Governing combination, calculation trace, assumptions, unsupported checks, stale-state hashes, and an order decision that cannot be green while a required stage is missing. |

The result state model is:

- `pass`: every prerequisite and the check itself are valid;
- `fail`: a valid check exceeds its criterion;
- `warning`: the check is valid but an engineering decision or sensitivity is
  outstanding;
- `not_checked`: the required calculation has not been implemented or run;
- `unsupported`: the selected member/detail cannot be checked by the active
  calculation pack;
- `blocked`: a prerequisite such as geometry, action mapping, stability,
  restraint, or connection definition is missing;
- `stale`: the result does not match the current project/model hash.

`not_checked`, `unsupported`, `blocked`, and `stale` are never green.

## Australian adaptation boundary

The first Australian standards pack will identify the project-selected
editions and amendments rather than hard-coding them into the generic graph.
The expected subject mapping is:

| Subject | Australian source family |
| --- | --- |
| Action combinations and general action rules | AS/NZS 1170.0 and project/NCC requirements |
| Permanent and imposed actions | AS/NZS 1170.1 plus measured or manufacturer mass data |
| Wind actions | AS/NZS 1170.2 plus the existing job/site capture and pressure calculation evidence |
| Snow where applicable | AS/NZS 1170.3 |
| Hot-rolled and welded steel members | AS 4100 |
| Cold-formed C/Z members, battens, purlins, and girts | AS/NZS 4600 plus validated manufacturer data |
| Steel connections and fasteners | Applicable AS 4100/AS/NZS 4600 provisions and product data |
| Concrete and anchorage | AS 3600, AS 5216, and validated anchor product data as applicable |
| Cladding and fixing performance | Applicable cladding standard and manufacturer span/fixing data |

P399 formulas that are explicitly tied to Eurocode resistance or UK National
Annex factors are reference evidence, not active Australian equations. P399's
frame-analysis and stability logic may be retained where its assumptions are
shown to be compatible with the selected analysis method and Australian design
basis.

## First migration slice

The next implementation is not another visual fixture. It is a parity slice
from the working calculator:

1. preserve a representative FBD job input and its calculation-sheet outputs;
2. port permanent actions, wind actions, and the actual FBD load combinations;
3. reproduce their intermediate values and final applied member/frame loads;
4. build the same elastic frame through the Tertius PyNite adapter;
5. compare nodes, releases, reactions, member end forces, moment/shear diagrams,
   and deflections against FBD and independent equilibrium;
6. add the P399 stability stage before activating any member pass/fail result;
7. only then port the relevant member and restraint checks.

The `structural_test` microcosm remains useful for geometry/graph/solver/viewer
tests. Its nominal `Zxe x fy` threshold is not a P399 or Australian member
verification and must not be presented as a design pass. It will remain a
renderer regression fixture until replaced by a verified calculation-pack
result.

## Current shed acceptance matrix

Before the shed can support an order decision, the matrix below must be complete.

| Changed item | Actions | Analysis/load path | Member/restraint effect | Connection/SLS effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Internal cladding and OSB | Measured/manufacturer mass, area and orientation | Reactions distributed to the supporting battens/girts/frames | Added axial, shear, moment and stability demand | Fixing demand and deflection compatibility |
| New C100 battens | Self-weight and tributary cladding/wind actions | Real spans, continuity, local axes, reactions and support points | AS/NZS 4600 section/member checks and compression-flange restraint | Screw/bolt checks and cladding serviceability |
| Revised wall layouts | Wall pressure and dead loads by actual panel | Actual frame/girt/stud/bracing topology | Changed unbraced lengths and load distribution | Changed connection actions and differential movement |
| Window and doors | Opening-dependent pressure and tributary areas | Jambs, headers/lintels, interrupted members and braces | Local member and stability checks around openings | Opening-frame connections and operational deflection limits |
| Roof and wall cladding | Inward and outward pressures plus permanent mass | Fastener-to-batten-to-frame path for every zone | Load reversal changes the compression flange and restraint case | Fastener pull-over/pull-out, sheet capacity and leakage/deflection criteria |
| Bases and anchors | Gravity thrust, shear and wind uplift/reversal | Declared base stiffness appropriate to ULS, stability and SLS analyses | Column result boundary condition | Bracket/base plate, anchors, concrete, edge distance and shear-transfer mechanism |

Impact from a falling branch and deliberate/controlled failure are separate
accidental-action and robustness studies. They cannot be inferred from a static
wind/dead-load utilisation or a nominal yield threshold.
